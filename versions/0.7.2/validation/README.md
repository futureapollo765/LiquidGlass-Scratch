# 0.7.2 — submenu return timing

Click the **Menu finish** header to fold the submenu back into its calling row. The center uses **open travel** and its size uses **open growth**, the same parameters as opening. Click outside or choose a leaf to minimize the entire menu to its launcher; the whole stack now uses **close travel** and **close growth**. No public input was added or changed. In the saved Studio example these values are 0.40 / 0.60 s for opening and Back, and 0.60 / 0.40 s for full dismissal. The bounce setting is 0.25.

[Watch both paths in the actual stage](menu-timing.mp4). The 207 frames come from the verified Studio export run through the installed TurboWarp renderer, at standard Scratch pen resolution and a simulated 30 Hz. The video captions identify the mouse action. [This frame](folding-submenu.png) shows the shared title partway back into the parent row.

- **Independent movement and growth:** a native fixture with deliberately different settings exercised a child and grandchild. At 200 ms on entry, both centers had traveled 79.97% while the height had grown 28.81%; on Back the matching distances reversed. A full dismissal selected the independent close values, including when dismissed by a leaf or outside during a partial transition. Zero times and velocity across a rapid reversal passed. Reopening the launcher during a full dismissal does not change the timing for already withdrawing children. Compare [submenu-timing.json](submenu-timing.json) with the [0.7.1 negative control](0.7.1-negative-control.json).
- **Ink and cleanup:** the shared original header and chevron keep their spring motion through transfer. Sixty-six generated palette squares are now native bitmaps drawn once per destination pixel at integer sprite positions. Before this fix the shared title could disappear in intermediate frames at normal pen resolution. Names and costume ordering are unchanged; original glyphs, photographic assets and optics are unchanged. Opacity, original artwork colors, native component imports, renderer presentation, and retention of the selected finish after closing two levels passed.
- **Size and provenance:** Studio project.json is 4,842,871 bytes and a settled open-child native save is 4,841,810 bytes. The importable sprite.json is 4,981,179 bytes. Both outputs reproduce byte-for-byte from the previous released archives preserved in ../inputs. The numbered 0.7.1 was not overwritten; neither open desktop project was replaced.
